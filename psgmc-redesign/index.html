Our Story

The Palm Springs Gay Men's Chorus was organized in 1999 as the Caballeros, a community chorus of twenty-four men inspired by a holiday concert from the Gay Men's Chorus of Los Angeles. Today, we are 100+ voices strong.

In 2018, Mayor Rob Moon and the City of Palm Springs honored us with a Proclamation as "The Official Gay Men's Chorus of the City of Palm Springs" — and April 8 was declared Palm Springs Gay Men's Chorus Day.

In 2019, 12 members sang with over 525 voices at Carnegie Hall in celebration of the 50th Anniversary of Stonewall.

⏳ Our Journey

Key Milestones

  • 1999 — The Caballeros debut with 24 members at a holiday concert
  • 2004 — First GALA Choruses Festival performance in Montreal
  • 2013 — Rebranded to Palm Springs Gay Men's Chorus
  • 2014 — Douglas C. Wilson elected Artistic Director
  • 2018 — Proclaimed the Official Gay Men's Chorus of Palm Springs
  • 2019 — Carnegie Hall debut for the 50th Anniversary of Stonewall
  • 2021 — Jerry R. Soria-Foust named Artistic Director
  • 2024 — 26th Season as inaugural Artists in Residence at the Plaza Theatre
"Through musical excellence, community outreach, and civic responsibility, the Palm Springs Gay Men's Chorus seeks to entertain, inspire, and educate the culturally diverse community in which we live."

💚 Giving Back

Community Impact

Since 1999, PSGMC has given back through programs that uplift LGBTQIA+ youth and our broader community.

George Zander Memorial Anti-Bullying Fund

Providing grants to Gay-Straight Alliances in Palm Springs Unified and Desert Sands school districts, empowering LGBTQ+ youth to build safe environments.

Chorus Cares

Collecting household items and toys for Martha's Village and Kitchen, running school supply drives, and supporting families across the Coachella Valley.

Community Performances

15+ outreach shows annually at DAP Health Equity Walk, Greater PS Pride, Cathedral City LGBT Days, Rainbow Youth Summit, and more.

Bruce Kenyon's
The Arrangement

One of only two 100% American-born musical categories in history — Barbershop harmony. Our 30-member ensemble delivers stunning four-part close harmony under award-winning director Bruce Kenyon.

Available for smaller private events, intimate gatherings, and community celebrations with a large repertoire of standards.
